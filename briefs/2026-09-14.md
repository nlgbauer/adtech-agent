# Adtech Market Brief — Week of 2026-09-14

## TL;DR — themes shaping the market this week
- **Antitrust is shifting from structure to conduct.** Google dodged a forced breakup; regulators are now chasing auction mechanics (FTC v. Amazon) and eyeing AI ad tooling next — expect behavioral fixes, not divestitures.
- **AI is rewriting the ad unit itself**, not just targeting — ChatGPT's click-to-chat format, Amazon's DSP inside ChatGPT, and agentic MMM tools all break the click→landing-page model advertisers still measure against.
- **Growth optimism and independent-adtech distress are diverging.** IAB raised its spend forecast and AI-driven ad dollars are climbing, yet The Trade Desk is down to a tenth of its 2024 peak — the growth is flowing to platform-native AI buying, not open-web DSPs.
- **Inventory keeps expanding faster than measurement or governance** — car dashboards, doctor's offices, FAST platforms hoarding audience data — supply-side land grabs are outpacing accountability.
- **Talent and account churn accelerating** in agencies as AI and trust erosion reshape who wins business and how (Publicis's pitch-free wins, Stagwell/Assembly reboot, OOH poaching from digital creative).
