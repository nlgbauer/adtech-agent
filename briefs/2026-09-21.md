# Adtech Market Brief — Week of 2026-09-21

## TL;DR — themes shaping the market this week
- **Google's antitrust remedies landed, and the market's verdict is "meh."** Publishers and buyers are recalibrating expectations downward — interoperability mandates look weak next to the structural breakup DOJ wanted.
- **Consolidation continues in the mid-tier, even as demand softens at the top.** Infillion and Taboola are rolling up niche data/network assets while The Trade Desk cuts 15% of staff — a bifurcated market rewarding scale plays and punishing growth-stock darlings.
- **Sell-side curation is emerging as the cookieless answer publishers actually want to sell.** Permutive/TransUnion is a bet that publisher data + third-party ID graphs beat clean rooms alone.
- **CTV and commerce measurement gaps are widening the trust deficit with buyers.** Better data isn't buying advertisers control — a pattern repeating in both video and the fragmenting retail/agentic commerce funnel.
- **Legacy law and AI-content litigation are quietly becoming the biggest compliance risk in the stack.** Decades-old privacy statutes and the OpenAI-NYT ruling both point toward retroactive liability nobody priced in.

## Notable moves

### Regulatory
- Google's ad tech antitrust remedies were unsealed, formalizing the AdX/DFP tying fix ordered by the court. ([AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/publishers/the-google-antitrust-remedies-are-too-little-too-late-but-publishers-will-take-what-they-can-get/))
  **Implication:** Publishers get a symbolic win, not real leverage — expect calls for stronger DOJ appeal or state-level follow-on action rather than a fundamentally reshaped market.
- Digiday examined whether interoperability mandates alone can loosen Google's grip on adland. ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/media-buying/can-adland-realistically-reduce-its-reliance-on-googles-vertically-integrated-ad-stack/))
  **Implication:** Skepticism is bipartisan across trade press — operators should plan multi-year diversification, not wait for court-forced share shifts.
- A New York court ruling found OpenAI's paywall violations undercut its fair-use defense in the NYT suit. ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/paywall-violation-eviscerates-fair-use-defense-what-openai-nyt-twist-means-for-publishers/))
  **Implication:** Strengthens publisher negotiating position on AI licensing deals industry-wide — expect harder terms in scraping/training agreements.
- Paramount Skydance settled a 12-state AG lawsuit tied to its WBD merger. ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/convergent-tv/paramount-gets-big-win-for-wbd-merger-after-settlement-news/))
  **Implication:** Clears a regulatory hurdle for the WBD deal, reinforcing that media megamergers are getting through despite antitrust noise elsewhere in the ecosystem.

### M&A
- Infillion acquired Foursquare, adding location data to its stack (terms undisclosed). ([AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/marketers/infillion-acquires-foursquare-adding-more-location-data-to-its-ever-growing-ad-tech-stack/))
  **Implication:** Signals continued fire-sale pricing for once-hot location/identity assets; Infillion positions itself as the default buyer for distressed adtech.
- Taboola made an offer to acquire finance-focused ad network Dianomi for ~£19M-£27M ($25M+ cash/earnout). ([AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/publishers/taboola-eyes-the-finance-vertical-with-an-offer-to-acquire-ad-network-dianomi/))
  **Implication:** Vertical-specific networks are cheap consolidation targets — watch for Taboola to keep buying niche premium-publisher relationships rather than build them.

### Company health
- The Trade Desk cut 15% of its workforce following two years of stock and leadership turmoil. ([AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/the-big-story/the-trade-desks-bumpy-ride-down/))
  **Implication:** The DSP category leader's stumble gives rivals (and Google's post-remedy exchange competitors) an opening — watch client retention closely.

### Launches
- Permutive integrated with TransUnion's identity graph to power sell-side curation. ([AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/technology/can-sell-side-curation-solve-the-cookieless-audience-problem-for-advertisers/))
  **Implication:** Publishers get a path to monetize first-party data without owning ID infrastructure — a template likely to be copied by other identity vendors.
- Pinterest launched Visual Search Ads and a broader ad-tools suite. ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/pinterest-unveils-new-suite-of-ad-tools-including-visual-search-ads-in-its-pitch-to-brands/))
  **Implication:** Pinterest is leaning on its younger, growing user base to pitch incremental budget — a hard sell without stronger measurement proof points.
- Walmart upgraded its Scintilla insights platform with marketplace data and deeper AI/Marty agent features. ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/walmart-adds-marketplace-data-deeper-ai-features-to-scintilla-insights-platform/))
  **Implication:** Retail media leaders are racing to own the measurement layer directly, squeezing third-party MMM/attribution vendors out of the retail data conversation.
- Meta launched a marketing hub to streamline creator-brand matchmaking with expanded APIs. ([Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/meta-streamlines-creator-brand-tie-ups-with-new-marketing-hub/830593/))
  **Implication:** Meta is vertically integrating creator commerce the way it did performance ads — reduces need for third-party creator marketplaces.

### Exec changes
- Accenture Song appointed Melissa Fein (ex-ANZ media lead) to scale U.S. media operations. ([Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/accenture-song-signals-us-media-ambitions-with-exec-appointment/830854/))
  **Implication:** Consultancies keep pushing further into media buying turf traditionally held by agency holding companies — pressure point for WPP/Omnicom/Dentsu on Coca-Cola-scale pitches.
