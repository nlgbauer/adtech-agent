# Adtech Market Brief — Week of 2026-07-27

## TL;DR — themes shaping the market this week
- **Google is bending, not breaking, its black-box grip.** Ground given on PMax controls and a "Buyer Direct" push signal Google trying to out-agent the agentic-AI wave rather than cede ground to it — control is being *offered*, not *lost*.
- **Retail media is hitting its adolescence.** Amazon's top sellers are in open revolt over margin and placement changes, Kroger installs a speed-focused Walmart vet as CEO, and "retail media goes wholesale" — the format is maturing from land-grab to power struggle.
- **CTV is quietly building the quality infrastructure it lacked.** IAB's Redefining Media Types Standard, WBD's pause-ad monetization, and Peacock's profitability (right before a Comcast spinoff) all point to streaming finally getting measurement and margin levers the open web never had.
- **Programmatic transparency has a ceiling.** DSPs champion openness in principle but resist activist scrutiny of their own supply chains in practice — a credibility gap that will keep surfacing as buy-side trust becomes a differentiator.
- **Agency and portfolio consolidation continues** as holdcos and media conglomerates simplify (Omnicom merging agencies, Comcast separating NBCU) — scale is being restructured, not abandoned.

## Notable moves

**Launches / Product**
- Google quietly loosens PMax black-box controls, giving buyers more input after years of criticism. [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media-buying/google-quietly-gives-ground-on-pmax-controls/?utm_campaign=digidaydis&utm_medium=rss&utm_source=general-rss)
  **Implication:** A concession, not a philosophy change — Google is ceding just enough control to blunt agentic-AI competitors' pitch of "you own the strategy."
- Warner Bros. Discovery builds proprietary pause-ad tech while waiting on programmatic standardization. [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/ctv-roundup/how-warner-bros-discovery-is-creating-value-out-of-dead-air-with-pause-ads/)
  **Implication:** Publishers aren't waiting for industry standards to monetize dead inventory — expect proprietary CTV formats to outpace standardized ones, complicating cross-platform buying.
- IAB advances a Redefining Media Types Standard to address streaming ad quality controls. [Digiday](https://digiday.com/future-of-tv/future-of-tv-briefing-the-streaming-ad-industry-finally-has-levers-to-pull-on-quality/?utm_campaign=digidaydis&utm_medium=rss&utm_source=general-rss)
  **Implication:** CTV finally gets a lever comparable to open-web viewability standards — a prerequisite for shifting more linear/upfront dollars into programmatic CTV.
- Michaels reports its Google Gemini-powered AI shopping assistant doubles conversion vs. traditional search. [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/michaels-claims-google-powered-ai-assistant-doubles-conversion-rate-of-traditional-search/?utm_campaign=digidaydis&utm_medium=rss&utm_source=general-rss)
  **Implication:** Early proof points like this will accelerate retailer adoption of AI-assistant commerce layers — but self-reported lift claims from a single retailer need independent verification before treating as benchmark.

**M&A / Corporate restructuring**

**Platform / Marketplace tension**

  **Implication:** As Amazon monetizes seller inventory more aggressively, its highest-value merchants are pushing back — a preview of the tension every retail media network will face as ad load rises.

**Programmatic governance**
